Source: https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx (commit a841596, v0.3.0), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Patch and Hugo Duprez.
Parts adapt Sprite Fusion's MIT effects (Hugo Duprez) and Hollow Crown (rehan-remade, MIT). The Patch Pixel font is NOT MIT and is not used.

Owner: sample comparison first ("ทำตัวอย่างเทียบก่อน"), recolour to the game's palette, Thai patterns later; then picked:
① hit spark (impacts/hit-spark), ② critical (impacts/crit-hit, shrink), ③ poison mist (nature/poison-cloud, shrink, keep the game's purple),
④ burning (status/burning), ⑤ frozen (status/frozen), ⑥ pickup (pickups/collect-burst), ⑦ warp (arcane/blink-out).
Not picked: ⑧ monster death poof (spawn/poof). Everything else keeps the PixelLab effects.

How (owner: "ออกเป็น sprite sheet เก็บใน assets/fx เหมือนของเดิม หลังงานกองมอน"):
- Export with `node tools/render.mjs <id> --sheet --opts '{"palette":[...]}'` from a pixelfx checkout outside the game repo;
  palettes are ramps taken from the existing effect each one replaces. Frames resampled to the old effect's fps (14–16), stored in
  assets/fx/ with manifest.json entries (cell, frames, fps, anchor, loop, "pixelfx": id + options) like the PixelLab ones.
- ④ ⑤ are drawn for PixelFX's stock character; they must be reworked to sit on our monsters (no stock-body silhouette left in the sheet).
- ② ③ shrunk to fit the game's scale (⚙ roughly no wider than 2× the hero for ②, about the old mist's width for ③); ③ stays purple.
- Licence: keep the MIT notice — `assets/fx/PIXELFX_LICENSE.txt` (LICENSE + NOTICE text) and a line in the game's credits/licence list.
- The PixelLab budget rule does not apply to these (no gen spent); CLAUDE.md notes the exception.
